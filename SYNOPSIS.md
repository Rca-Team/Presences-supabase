## 🏫 About the Project

**Presences** is an intelligent, AI-powered attendance management system designed for schools, colleges, and educational institutions. It replaces the traditional and error-prone method of calling out names or signing registers with a seamless, fully automated system that uses a student's face as their identity. Built with the modern needs of institutions in mind, Presences is not just an attendance tool — it is a complete ecosystem for managing students, teachers, parents, and institutional safety.

---

## ❗ The Problem

### 1. Traditional Attendance is Broken
In most schools and colleges across India, attendance is still marked manually — either by calling names in class, maintaining paper registers, or using basic spreadsheets. This system is:
- **Slow and time-consuming** — a 5–10 minute process every period just to mark names
- **Prone to proxy attendance** — students marking absent friends as present (buddy punching)
- **Inaccurate** — human errors in data entry lead to wrong records
- **Hard to report** — generating attendance reports requires significant manual effort

### 2. Parents Are Left in the Dark
Parents have no real-time visibility into whether their child actually attended school that day. They rely on the student's word, which creates trust gaps and safety concerns — especially in cases of bunking or missing students.

### 3. Institutions Lack Safety Infrastructure
Most schools have no centralized emergency alert system. In case of a fire, earthquake, medical emergency, or security lockdown, there is no unified, instant communication tool to reach all staff and students simultaneously.

### 4. No Actionable Insights for Administrators
Principals and administrators lack real-time dashboards showing attendance trends, low-attendance students, department-wise performance, or historical data — making it impossible to take timely action.

### 5. Gate Security is Unmonitored
School gates are typically manned by a single guard with no smart monitoring — anyone can enter or exit without being tracked, and there is no automated record of who came in or went out.

---

## ✅ Our Solution — Presences

Presences solves every one of these problems by bringing together **Artificial Intelligence**, **facial recognition**, **real-time cloud data**, and **multi-user portals** into one unified platform.

Instead of calling names, the system simply *looks* at the student and marks them present — instantly, accurately, and automatically.

## 🌟 Key Features

### 1. 🤖 Facial Recognition Attendance
The core of Presences. Students are registered once by capturing their face. From that point forward, whenever they appear in front of the camera, the system automatically recognizes them and marks their attendance — no touching, no signing, no calling of names. This eliminates proxy attendance entirely, since a face cannot be faked.

### 2. 🚪 Gate Mode — Smart Entry & Exit Tracking
A dedicated gate monitoring mode uses the live camera feed at the school entrance. As students pass through the gate, they are automatically recognized and logged. The system:
- Detects whether the person is **recognized or a stranger**
- Flags **late arrivals** with timestamps
- Monitors **entry and exit flow** in real time
- Alerts staff about **unrecognized individuals** entering the premises

This transforms the school gate into an intelligent, always-on security checkpoint.

### 3. 👁️ Uniform Compliance Monitoring
The Gate Vision mode goes a step further — it can detect whether a student entering campus is wearing the correct school uniform. Non-compliant students are flagged automatically, reducing the burden on gate staff and ensuring discipline without confrontation.

### 4. 🧠 Jarvis — AI Intelligent Assistant
Presences includes **Jarvis**, an AI-powered administrative assistant. Jarvis can:
- Audit the entire system and generate a **health score**
- Answer questions about student records, attendance gaps, and biometric data coverage
- Give voice-based responses and can be queried via a conversational chat interface
- Detect anomalies and recommend corrective actions
- Perform **self-healing diagnostics** to fix data inconsistencies automatically

Jarvis makes the system self-aware — it does not just store data, it understands it.

### 5. 👨‍👩‍👧 Parent Portal — Real-Time Child Monitoring
Parents can log in using their child's student ID and registered phone number to access a dedicated portal that shows:
- **Daily and monthly attendance calendar** — see exactly which days were present or absent
- **Live timetable** — know which class the child is currently attending
- **Gate entry and exit logs** — see when the child entered and left school
- **Leave request management** — apply for leave directly through the portal
- **Faculty directory** — contact teachers easily
- **Achievement badges** — track the child's milestones and recognition
- **Emergency gate pass requests** — request early dismissal from within the app

This gives parents complete peace of mind and transparent communication with the institution.

### 6. 🚨 Emergency Alert System
A real-time emergency broadcast system that instantly pushes alerts to every connected device in the institution. Supported emergency types include:
- 🔥 **Fire Alarm** — with evacuation instructions
- 🔒 **Lockdown** — with shelter-in-place guidance
- 🚪 **Evacuation** — with assembly point directions
- 🌍 **Earthquake** — with Drop-Cover-Hold instructions
- 🏥 **Medical Emergency** — alerting first-aid teams

All alerts come with sound alarms, visual indicators, and step-by-step on-screen instructions.

### 7. 📊 Admin Dashboard & Analytics
School administrators get access to a powerful control panel featuring:
- Department-wise and class-wise attendance breakdowns
- Low-attendance student alerts
- Historical attendance trends with visual charts
- Student and staff management
- Data export to PDF, Excel, and printable report cards

### 8. 🪪 QR-Based Gate Pass System
Students or parents can generate a QR-based **digital gate pass** for approved early exits or late entries. The gate scanning system reads these QR codes instantly, providing a paperless and forgery-resistant pass system.

### 9. 📱 Mobile App (Android & iOS)
Presences is available as a full mobile application on both Android and iOS. It also works as a **Progressive Web App (PWA)** — meaning it can be installed on any device like a native app. It even supports **offline mode** for basic functionality when the internet is unavailable.

### 10. 🔔 Push Notifications
Staff and parents receive real-time push notifications for important events — such as a student marked absent, a gate alert, or an emergency broadcast — even when the app is not open.

### 11. 🔐 Secure Role-Based Access
The system supports multiple user roles — **Admin, Teacher, Student, and Parent** — each with their own tailored view and permissions. Data is protected with industry-standard security practices.
